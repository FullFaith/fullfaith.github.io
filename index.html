Privacy and cookies

Last updated 8 October 2026

FullFaith MTÜ (registry code 80677142) is a nonprofit in Estonia. We are responsible for the personal data described on this page. You can reach us at fullfaithproduction@gmail.com.

The short version

We set no cookies, run no analytics and show no ads. We only keep what we need to reply to you, thank you for a gift and keep our accounts.

What we keep and why

  • Emails and messages. If you write to us, we keep your name, email and what you tell us, so we can reply and run the project. We keep them while we work together and for a reasonable time afterwards, then delete them.
  • Card gifts. Stripe processes the payment, and we never see your card details. Stripe gives us your name, email, the amount and the date. We use them to thank you and to keep accounting records, which Estonian law requires us to keep for several years.
  • Bank gifts. Your bank shows us your name, account number, the amount and your message. We use them in the same way.
  • Paid video work. For clients we keep contact details, invoices and project files for the project and for our accounts.

We do this to answer you, to carry out agreements, to follow accounting law, and because it is how a small nonprofit has to run.

Other companies that see your visit

When you open this site, these services receive your IP address and browser details, as any website host or font provider does:

  • GitHub hosts this site. Their privacy statement
  • Google Fonts loads our typefaces. Google's privacy policy
  • YouTube supplies the thumbnails for our films. Videos play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and only after you press play, but YouTube may still collect data once a video plays.

If you choose to give, you leave this site for Stripe (privacy policy) or DonationBox, and their own policies apply.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Stripe, DonationBox and YouTube may set their own cookies when you use them.

Photos and films

If you appear in one of our films or photos and want it removed, email us and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to limit how we use it, or to object to it. Email us and we will answer within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee).

Terms

Last updated 8 October 2026

These terms cover work by FullFaith MTÜ (registry code 80677142). We keep them short and plain. We agree the details of each project with you in writing before we start, and email counts as writing. If a quote says something different, the quote wins for that project.

Free and paid work

Our work is free for projects, events and churches that connect directly to Christianity. That means a church, or someone who uses the work to spread the gospel or the money to fund a church. A normal business is not free, even if it is run by Christians. If you are not sure which side you fall on, ask us.

We decide whether a project is free, and we can say no to a free project. On a free project we do not set a price. You are welcome to give a donation instead. We do ask you to cover anything we have to buy or pay for to make the project work, such as transport, props or a music licence.

Quotes and payment

Paid work starts with a written quote. The prices on the Services page are starting prices, and the quote gives the real one. Payment is usually after delivery. We send an invoice when the work is delivered, and it shows the due date. If a project needs something different, the quote says so.

How long it takes

A simple project usually takes about a week. A harder one takes a couple of weeks, and the biggest can take up to a month. We agree a date with you before we start.

Changes

Each quote or agreement says how many rounds of changes are included. If you need more, we will talk it through before doing the extra work.

Ownership and showing the work

We talk about who owns the finished video with every client before we start. We usually hope to keep the right to show the work on our website and social media. If you ask for the raw footage, you can have it.

Music and your material

If a project needs licensed music, the licence is paid for by the client. If you give us music, photos or other material, you are responsible for having the right to use it.

Cancelling

If you need to cancel or move a date, tell us as early as you can. If we have already bought or paid for something for your project, we will ask you to cover it.

If something goes wrong

We would always rather talk it through first. Estonian law applies to these terms. How we handle your personal data is on the privacy page.
